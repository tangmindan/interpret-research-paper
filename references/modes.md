# Operating modes

Mode controls depth, not scientific honesty. Use the smallest mode that answers the request.

## quick

For triage. Record canonical identity, source hash, contribution, question, cohort/data, method outline, headline findings, principal figures, one major limitation, and unresolved checks. Skip exhaustive panels, supplements, presentations, and external novelty checks unless requested.

## standard

For a reusable research note. Add exact Methods behind major results, all main figures with visual QA, result-relevant panels, statistical unit/test/multiplicity/nesting checks, observation–interpretation–boundary claim records, code/data availability, and critical appraisal. This is the default.

## deep

For journal club, peer review, implementation, or public artifacts. Add every relevant main panel, essential supplements, robustness and alternative analyses, causal and translation boundaries, requested external novelty verification, complete candidate-image QA, reproduction-oriented method/statistics teaching, and cross-artifact checks.

## Transitions

- `quick -> standard -> deep` is additive: reuse verified evidence and compute only missing fields.
- A shallower deliverable may reuse deeper evidence without deleting it.
- Store requested and completed mode separately.
- Unknown information remains unknown in every mode.
